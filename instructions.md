**Use a unigram or a bigram**

You can enter a unigram, i.e., a single term, e.g., marketing, privacy, profit. You can also enter a bigram, i.e., two related terms separated by a space, e.g., profit margin.
The app will display the most related terms to the entered focal term, providing a comprehensive exploration of connections.

**Troubleshooting**

Consider trying different combinations if the app does not return results for your bigram. Not all bigrams may yield related terms, so we recommend experimenting with variations.
